01 / THE FRICTION

MORE THAN
THE SYLLABUS.

Growing up within Korea's educational expectations can leave little room for interests that don't fit the next test, grade, or prescribed path. We know what it's like to want to try more than the schedule allows.

02 / OUR RESPONSE

START
ANYWAY.

We make space for people to learn, experiment, build, write, research, or create on their own terms. A finished product isn't required; curiosity and the effort to pursue it are enough to begin.

03 / OUR PRINCIPLE

TOGETHER.
NOT THE SAME.

We don't have to share a major, career plan, opinion, or interest. We can support each other without becoming one voice—and choose to collaborate when our paths genuinely meet.
