01 / ABOUT THE COLLECTIVE

What is
Stupid Idiots?

A small, independent collective. Different people, individual projects, and a shared space for our work.

Our members follow their own interests and mostly work independently. We also come together on shared projects, with credit given to the people involved.

This site brings that work together: what we’re making, who’s behind it, and how our projects develop.
