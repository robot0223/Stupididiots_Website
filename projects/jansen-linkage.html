Jansen Linkage

Experiments with the geometry and movement of a walking mechanism.

Experimenting

About the project

An exploration of how connected mechanical parts create a walking motion. The project works through linkage geometry, CAD, motion studies, and the challenges of simulating a walking mechanism.

What it explores

Geometry
Exploring the relationships between the connected parts of a walking mechanism.
CAD & motion studies
Using models to examine the mechanism and its movement.
Simulation
Working through the challenges of reproducing that motion in simulation.
