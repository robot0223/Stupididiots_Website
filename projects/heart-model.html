About the project

An educational prototype that explores how pumping and fluid circulation can be represented in a physical model. The project looks at the mechanical behavior of the heart through making and experimentation.

What it explores

Pumping
Exploring the mechanical action of moving fluid through a model.
Circulation
Examining fluid circulation as part of a connected system.
Physical modeling
Making an educational prototype to explore the behavior of the heart.
