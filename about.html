Stupid Idiots is an independent collective. We come together around a shared vision, with room for each person to follow their own interests.

We're together because of what we believe in. That doesn't mean becoming copies of one another. Different projects and ways of thinking are part of what makes this space ours.

Some of the work here is software. Some explores mechanisms or physical models. Each project belongs to the person behind it, and each is another way to follow a question.

What we believe

Living within Korea's educational expectations can leave little room for interests beyond the next test, grade, or prescribed route. We believe those interests deserve room, too.

Beyond the syllabus

There's more to learn, make, and question than any prescribed path can contain. This is a place for those other interests.

Start anyway

Learning, experimenting, researching, writing, and building are worth doing before there's a finished product to show. Curiosity is a reason to begin.

One voice, many ways

We share a point of view without following identical paths. Different interests give that vision more ways to take shape.
